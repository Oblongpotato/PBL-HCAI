A recommender that has never met you has to learn your taste from a handful of questions. It could show you two films and ask which you prefer, or show you ten and ask you to put them in order. Which is the better use of your attention is an empirical question, and this is the study that would answer it.

The study is designed, not run. What follows is the protocol and a working participant interface that could be used to carry it out.

Read the design

The film representation, the extension of Bradley–Terry to rankings, and the full experimental protocol: hypotheses, sample size, procedure, analysis, ethics and threats to validity.
