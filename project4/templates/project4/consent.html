What this is

You will be asked about films twice, in two different ways. First you will choose between pairs; later you will put a list of ten in order, or the other way round. It takes about fifteen minutes. At the end you will see recommendations worked out from your answers.

What is recorded

  • The orderings you give, and how long each one took.
  • A random identifier for your session.

No name, no email address, no IP address. There is nowhere in the database for a personal detail to go. Nothing recorded could be traced back to you.

Your choices

Taking part is voluntary and you can stop at any point by closing the page, without giving a reason. Because the session is anonymous, data already submitted cannot be picked out and removed afterwards, which is why this is said before you begin rather than after.

This is a course exercise demonstrating a study design. It is not collecting research data and the results are not being analysed or published.
