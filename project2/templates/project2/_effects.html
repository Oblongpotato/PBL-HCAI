{% if exact_available %} The dotted overlay is the exact derivative: for a multinomial logistic model ∂P₁/∂x₌ = P₁(w₁₌ − Σₖ Pₖwₖ₌), corrected for the standardisation applied before fitting. It agrees with the discretised estimate, which is the check that the implementation is right. {% else %} A decision tree is piecewise constant: its derivative is zero almost everywhere, with jumps at the split thresholds.
