5. Learning what the expert is good at

Section 4 assumed we already knew, for every training article, whether the expert would have been right. Collecting that means asking a human about all {{ results.data.n_train }} of them. Here we start with none of it and may only ask a few, so the question is which articles to ask about.

The strategy targets the decision the answers feed. Deferral is hardest to call where the expert and the classifier are equally likely to be right, so articles whose estimated probabilities nearly tie are the informative ones. That alone chases outliers, so it is multiplied by how representative the article is of the rest of the pool.
