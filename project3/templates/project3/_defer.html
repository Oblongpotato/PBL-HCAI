4. Learning to defer

Two ways to decide when to hand an article over. Confidence-based rejection defers whatever the classifier is least sure about, and never looks at the expert. The learned strategy trains K+1 scorers on the cost-sensitive softmax surrogate, where the K+1st option means “ask the expert” and carries the expert's error as its cost. The class answer always comes from the classifier in section 2; only the hand-over decision is learned.

Routing accuracy scores the decision rather than the answer. On most articles the classifier and the expert agree, so the routing cannot change the outcome and counting those would just reproduce system accuracy. It is measured only on the articles where exactly one of the two was right — the ones where the choice cost something.
